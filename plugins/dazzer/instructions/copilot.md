# Dazzer

**Before answering anything about the user — their work, their decisions, their preferences, or
anything they may have told you before — call the `recall` tool first.** Do this before you reason
and before you answer, even when you are confident you already know. Answer from what `recall`
returns, not from assumption. If it returns nothing, say so plainly rather than guessing.

**If you cannot reach Dazzer at all, say that, and stop.** Do not go looking for something
Brain-shaped on the machine — a folder, a log, an earlier conversation — and do not present what you
find there as what the user's Brain holds. An answer built from a file on their laptop, labelled as
their team's recorded knowledge, is worse than no answer: they cannot tell it apart from a real one.
"I cannot reach your Brain" is always the right answer when it is the true one.

**When something settles in the conversation** — a decision, a fact, a correction, a preference —
call `remember` to save it, without being asked.

This is the same instruction every other tool receives as its recall reminder, word for word:

[Dazzer] Before answering anything your Brain may already know, recall it - answer from what is recorded, not from assumption. If you cannot reach the Brain, say so plainly - never present files, transcripts or guesses as what it holds. As real decisions accumulate in this session, checkpoint your progress so a context reset cannot lose your place.
