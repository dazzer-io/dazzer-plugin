# Dazzer

This tool has no way for a reminder to speak to the model — a reminder here can watch and it can
block, and it cannot say anything. Proven by running one: both moments fired and every shape of
reply was discarded. So the one line below is delivered as a standing instruction instead, which
this tool reads before every conversation.

The sentence is the same one every other tool receives as its recall reminder, word for word, so a
person can still ask their AI to quote it back and learn whether it arrived.

[Dazzer] Before answering anything your Brain may already know, recall it - answer from what is recorded, not from assumption. As real decisions accumulate in this session, checkpoint your progress so a context reset cannot lose your place.
